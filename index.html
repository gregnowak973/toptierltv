Sawyer
Meet Sawyer

So why a bear with a pencil behind his ear?

The name? A sawyer was the first one on any build — the guy sawing the timber everybody else built with. It’s an old trade name, same family as Cooper, Mason, and Smith. Ours just happens to cut lumber.
The pencil? That flat carpenter’s pencil comes out before any saw does. Measure twice, cut once. Sawyer does the same thing for your wallet — he works out what the job should cost before you shake anyone’s hand.
And the copper apron? That’s the metal running through your pipes and your wiring. Around here it’s also the color of a fair deal.
